# Bylaw changelog

Semver per R-PREC-05: major = a previously green site now fails a gate; minor = new rule with a new gate; patch = wording.

## 0.6.0 — 2026-10-18 (contract written; the catalog catches up in Tasks 2–8 of the phase-7 plan)

Flair. The motion level becomes a flair dial with four tokens and an enabled-device list per level (R-TOK-12). Keyframe list grows to eighteen: `reveal-settle`, `pop`, `progress`, `parallax-sm/md/lg`, `drift`, `settle` (R-TOK-13). Entrance travel up to 48 px, three groups, two depth layers (R-MOT-03). Reveals are a preset table instead of one exact string, with the item range `cover 5% → cover 25%` shifted 3 % per column; the entrance sits on the wrapper, the hover on the inner element (R-MOT-05, R-MOT-07). Reading-progress hairline (R-MOT-08, R-MOT-14). Count-up v2 from build-time attributes with reserved width and stagger (R-MOT-10). Depth at identity at rest is allowed on hero media and glows (R-MOT-11). New R-MOT-15 Depth and R-MOT-16 Entrance vocabulary. Sticky editorial row on the rhythm scale (R-LAY-02). `consistency/motion-vocabulary` planned (R-GATE-13). D46–D52. Minor by R-PREC-05: the new rules are manual until the check lands, and a 0.5.0 site keeps its green gates. Until Tasks 2–8 land, the reference site still shows the 0.5.0 motion; the bylaws describe where it is going.

## 0.4.0 — 2026-10-18

Motion and style. `engine/DESIGN.md` records the design intent. Motion presets 12/24/32 px (R-TOK-12); keyframes `reveal-end` and `rise-in` (R-TOK-13); fixed hover vocabulary (R-MOT-07); overlay entrances with `@starting-style` and blurred backdrops (R-MOT-06); header shadow scroll-driven (R-MOT-08); smooth scrolling and floating back-to-top (R-MOT-13); scroll-driven appearance (R-MOT-14). Rhythm scale enforced by `consistency/rhythm` (R-LAY-02); reflow enforced by `a11y/reflow` (R-LAY-05). Button variants and effects (R-COMP-05); native `video` component (R-COMP-12); video budget (R-PERF-17); generated sample clip (R-ASSET-13). Gradients and accent placements enumerated (R-COL-02, R-COL-09). D31–D34. Home pages of Latin-script locales get a 64 KB HTML budget (R-PERF-01, D36). Review pass (D37): transition lists name `translate` and `scale` (R-MOT-01, R-MOT-06, R-MOT-07), the script fallback is scoped to `nosda:` (R-MOT-04, R-MOT-05), items stagger by range shift with `both` fill and the `--reveal-stagger` token is dropped (R-MOT-03, R-MOT-05, R-TOK-12), `@utility` blocks for R-COL-09 backgrounds are allowed (R-PREC-07). The two new checks (`consistency/rhythm`, `a11y/reflow`) did fail the reference site before its fixes, which R-PREC-05 would call a major change; this stays 0.4.0 because no site outside this repository has been built against 0.3.0. The a11y gate runs axe-core directly through puppeteer-core on one shared browser, four pages at a time, with per-page timeouts and a skip for self-redirecting pages (R-GATE-09, D38; pa11y dropped); `npm run serve -- --dev` serves without caching for hand testing.
