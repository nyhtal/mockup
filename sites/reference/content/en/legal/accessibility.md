## Our commitment

This site aims to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines <a href="https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">(WCAG) 2.2<svg aria-hidden="true" class="ms-1 inline size-4 align-[-0.125em]"><use href="#i-external-link"></use></svg><span class="sr-only"> (opens in a new tab)</span></a> at level AA. Every change is checked with automated tests and a keyboard and screen reader walkthrough before it is published.

## What works

- Every page can be used with a keyboard alone.
- Text can be enlarged to 200% and the layout reflows at 320 pixels wide.
- Colour contrast meets the AA ratios in both light and dark schemes.
- Motion respects your reduced-motion setting.

## Known limits

Map and video placeholders open the third-party service only after you choose to, and that service's accessibility is theirs.

## Tell us

If anything on this site is hard to use, write to accessibility@example.com and we reply within two working days with a fix or a workaround.
