## The service

A subscription gives you access to Studio, Edge and Cloud for the lines and sites in your order, with support as described on the pricing page.

## Your responsibilities

You are responsible for the line descriptions you write, for reviewing generated logic before it runs, and for the safety of your plant. Northwind generates and tests logic; it does not certify lines.

## Availability

Cloud is run to a 99.9% monthly uptime target. Edge and Studio run on your hardware and do not depend on Cloud to keep a line running.

## Liability

Our liability under these terms is limited to the fees you paid in the twelve months before the claim. Nothing limits liability that cannot be limited by law.

## Ending the agreement

Monthly tiers end at the end of any month on notice. We may suspend the service for unpaid fees after thirty days' notice. You can export descriptions and logs at any time, including after the end.
