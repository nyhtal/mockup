## Necessary

One cookie remembers your cookie choice for twelve months. It holds no personal data.

## Analytics

If you accept analytics, a cookie counts page views without identifying you. It expires after thirteen months.

## Marketing

If you accept marketing, a cookie lets us measure whether a campaign page led to a demo request. It expires after ninety days.

## Changing your choice

Open the cookie preferences from the banner or from the link in the footer, and change any switch. The change applies at once.
