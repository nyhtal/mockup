## Who we are

Northwind Dynamics Ltd, 4 Canal Street, Manchester M1 3HE, United Kingdom, is the controller for personal data collected on this site and in the products.

## What we collect

- Contact forms: your name, work email, company and message, to answer you.
- Newsletter: your email address and the preferences you choose.
- Products: user names and the change log entries you create, to run the service.
- This site: no analytics cookies unless you accept them on the cookie banner.

## Why and for how long

We use contact details to reply and, if you asked for it, to send product updates. Newsletter data is kept until you unsubscribe. Product data is kept for the life of your subscription and deleted ninety days after it ends.

## Who can see it

Northwind staff who need it for their work, and the hosting provider in the EU. We do not sell data and we do not share it with advertisers.

## Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, correct it, or have it deleted by writing to privacy@example.com. We reply within one month.

## Where data is kept

### Hosting in the EU

Descriptions, logs and account data are stored in two data centres in Frankfurt.

#### Backups

A copy is taken every night and kept, encrypted, in the second data centre.

##### How long backups are kept

Thirty days. A deleted record leaves the backups within thirty days of its deletion.

###### Legal holds

If a court orders us to keep a record, we keep it for as long as the order lasts and tell you, unless the order forbids it.
