# Sound Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** A fuller, rule-bound set of synthesised UI sounds (hover, press, menus, accordions, dialogs, steps, toggles, sliders, copy, submit, entrances), loaded only for visitors who switch sound on, with a bylaw and a style-guide section that auditions every sound.

**Architecture:** A new ES module `engine/scripts/sound.js` (≤ 4608 B) owns the audio context, a three-voice mixer, the vocabulary and its own document listeners; it exports one function `S(name, force, el, ctx)`. `enhance.js` drops its inline synthesiser, keeps the toggle state, imports `./sound.js` once when sound is on (or for a style-guide audition), and calls `S` at the three points only it sees: an entrance after the first report, a copy that succeeded, the toggle itself. `<html>` carries `data-motion` so the module can scale with the flair level.

**Tech Stack:** Web Audio API (OscillatorNode, AudioBufferSourceNode, BiquadFilterNode, GainNode), hand-minified ES modules (like `enhance.js`), Eleventy/Nunjucks templates, node:test, puppeteer-core with the local Chrome.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-23-sound-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- Sound stays OFF by default; the choice lives in `sessionStorage.sound` for the tab; no cookie; `localStorage` holds only `theme`.
- No audio files; every sound is synthesised.
- `enhance.js` ≤ 5120 B; `sound.js` ≤ 4608 B (both as shipped; both hand-minified, no build-time minifier exists).
- No page's HTML references `sound.js`; it is fetched only by `import("./sound.js")` from `enhance.js`.
- No audio context is created on load; the module creates or resumes it only inside a trusted `pointerdown`/`keydown`, or adopts the one `enhance.js` creates synchronously inside the enabling or audition click.
- Pitches from C major pentatonic: C4 262, G4 392, C5 523, D5 587, E5 659, G5 784 Hz. Master gain .6 (calm .48), lowpass 6 kHz, no reverb, no panning.
- At most three voices; `hover` and `in` are ambient and are the first to be stolen; an ambient cue never steals a direct one.
- Silent: count-up, validation errors, navigation, loading, the toast appearing, video play, keyboard focus moving, disabled controls, a hidden tab.
- Every cue is the same in every locale and in RTL.
- Bylaws release 0.21.0 (minor, new bylaw `20-sound.md`, rule ids `R-SND-NN`).

## Review Focus

- A fast mouse sweep across the mega-menu bar must not chatter: one hover per 250 ms, once per target, after 100 ms dwell. → Task 1 test "hover: dwell, throttle and once per target".
- An exclusive `<details name>` group (tabs, FAQ) must play only the open, never the sibling's close; a popover replaced by another must not play `unpop`. → Task 1 test "exclusive groups and replaced popovers play only the open".
- Lightbox next/previous (click and arrow keys) must sound as one `step`, not `unsheet` + `sheet`. → Task 1 test "lightbox next is one step".
- Turning sound off mid-sound and then hovering must be silent at once, and the `off` cue itself must still play. → Task 1 test "off plays, then silence".
- A visitor whose tab restored "on" must hear nothing until their first click or key press, and nothing at load. → Task 1 test "restored on: silent until the first gesture".

- Produces: `export const S = (k, f, e, c) => void` in `sound.js`. `k` cue name (`hover press pop unpop open close sheet unsheet step on off up down detent copy submit in`); `f` force (1 = play even while sound is off, skip calm/reduced-motion filters: used by the toggle and the style-guide audition); `e` the element the cue is about (used by `press` for the primary variant and `in` for its contour); `c` an `AudioContext` created by `enhance.js` in a click, adopted if the module has none. `S("on")` sets the module's state on; `S("off")` cancels every voice, plays `off`, then sets it off.
- Produces in `enhance.js`: `S(k, f, e)` wrapper = `(on||f)&&(X||=import("./sound.js")).then(m=>m.S(k,f,e,A),()=>{})`; `A` the context it creates in the enabling or audition click.
- Produces in the DOM: `[data-sound-demo="<cue>"]` buttons (Task 3 renders them; Task 1's fixture uses one).

```markdown
# 20 Sound

Purpose: the site's opt-in UI sounds: when each plays, what it sounds like, how loud, and what stays silent, so sound on any site built on the engine is one quiet family that confirms what the visitor does and never competes with the content. Sources: https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Web_Audio_API/Best_practices, https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/Media/Guides/Autoplay, https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/AudioParam/exponentialRampToValueAtTime.

### R-SND-01 Off by default, opt-in per tab
MUST. Sound is off until the visitor presses a sound toggle (`[data-sound]`, `aria-pressed`, named "Sound effects"; in the footer on every page, in the header from `xl`, in the drawer below `lg`). The choice lives in `sessionStorage.sound` for the tab; no cookie, no `localStorage`. Restoring "on" from the session plays nothing.
Why: A site that makes noise unasked is not professional; a choice that outlived the tab would surprise the visitor next time.
Gate: manual: checklists/pre-delivery.md

### R-SND-02 The sound module
MUST. Every sound is synthesised with the Web Audio API in `engine/scripts/sound.js`, an ES module of at most 4608 bytes with no dependencies and no audio files, which `enhance.js` imports only after the visitor turns sound on (or presses an audition button, R-SND-11). No page references it, so a visitor who leaves sound off never downloads it. It exports one function, `S(name, force, element, context)`.
Why: Most visitors never switch sound on; they should not pay for it.
Gate: budget/js

### R-SND-03 The vocabulary
MUST. The cues are these and no others. Pitches come from C major pentatonic (C4 262, G4 392, C5 523, D5 587, E5 659, G5 784 Hz) so overlaps agree; every voice rises from .0001 to its peak in 4 ms (25 ms for noise) and decays exponentially to .0001 by its length; gains are per voice before a master gain of .6 and a 6 kHz lowpass.

| Cue | Plays when | Recipe |
|---|---|---|
| `hover` | the mouse rests 100 ms on `a[href]`, an enabled `button`, a `summary` or a `label` | sine G5, 24 ms, .008 |
| `press` | a plain button or button-styled link is pressed (pointer down, or a keyboard activation) | triangle E5→C5, 35 ms, .02; primary .026 |
| `pop` / `unpop` | a popover opens / closes (menus, language switcher, tooltip) | sine C5→E5 45 ms .02 / E5→C5 35 ms .014 |
| `open` / `close` | a `<details>` opens / closes (accordion, FAQ, tabs, feature disclosures) | sine C5→E5 85 ms .018 / E5→C5 65 ms .012 |
| `sheet` / `unsheet` | a dialog opens / closes (modal, drawer, lightbox, contact launcher) | sine C4→G4 120 ms .028 / G4→C4 90 ms .02; at `expressive` plus noise bandpassed at 900 Hz (Q .7), .004 |
| `step` | a carousel snaps to a new item; the lightbox moves to the next or previous picture | triangle E5, 40 ms, .018 |
| `on`·`up` / `off`·`down` | the sound toggle (`on`/`off`, which also switch the state) and the theme toggle (`up`/`down`) | C5 then E5 / E5 then C5, 40 ms each, 45 ms apart, .022 |
| `detent` | a range input passes a tenth of its range | sine C5, 18 ms, .009 |
| `copy` | the clipboard write resolved | E5 then G5, 45 ms each, 55 ms apart, .025 |
| `submit` | a form's `submit` event fires | C5 then G5, 65 ms each, 75 ms apart, .03 |
| `in` | an entrance starts after the first report (R-MOT-05) | noise bandpassed at 900 Hz (Q .7), 180 ms, .005; at `expressive` a rising group adds sine C5→D5 and a settling one D5→C5, 55 ms, .007 |

Why: A fixed, small family is what makes the sound read as one product, the way the motion table does for movement.
Gate: manual: checklists/new-component.md

### R-SND-04 One action, one sound
MUST. A control that opens something (`popovertarget`, `commandfor`, a `summary`) plays the open cue and not `press`; a submit button plays `submit` (when the form really submits) and not `press`; a `<details>` closing because a sibling of its `name` group opened, and a popover closing because another opened, are silent; the lightbox's next and previous play one `step`, not `unsheet` and `sheet`; nothing plays for something that failed (an invalid form, a rejected copy).
Why: Two sounds for one click is noise, and a success sound for a failure is a lie.
Gate: manual: checklists/new-component.md

### R-SND-05 Mixer and limits
MUST. At most three voices sound at once; a two-note cue counts as two. `hover` and `in` are ambient: a direct cue that needs a voice cuts the oldest ambient one with a 5 ms fade, then the oldest voice; an ambient cue that finds no ambient voice to cut is dropped. Rate limits: `hover` one per 250 ms, `in` one per 1.2 s, `step` and `detent` one per 125 ms.
Why: Limits are what keep a sweep across a menu or a page of cards from turning into chatter.
Gate: manual: checklists/pre-delivery.md

### R-SND-06 Pointer, keyboard and touch
MUST. `hover` plays only for a mouse on a device that matches `(hover:hover) and (pointer:fine)`, only after real pointer movement, after 100 ms of dwell, and once per target until the pointer leaves it. Keyboard focus moving is silent; activating a control by keyboard plays its cue. Touch gets the action cues only.
Why: A tick on every Tab is a penalty for keyboard users; hover sounds on touch would fire on taps that are already actions.
Gate: manual: checklists/new-component.md

### R-SND-07 Entrances
MUST. `enhance.js` asks for `in` for each entrance it reveals after its first report; the module's rate limit turns a section's cascade into one breath. Nothing that is in view at load makes a sound, and no card, item or count has a sound of its own.
Why: The breath accompanies the motion the visitor caused by scrolling; per-item sounds would be a xylophone.
Gate: manual: checklists/pre-delivery.md

### R-SND-08 Reduced motion and the motion level
MUST. `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` silences `in` and keeps the action cues (it is a motion preference, not an audio one). The page carries the flair level as `data-motion` on `<html>`: `calm` drops `hover` and `in` and sets the master gain to .48; `standard` is the table; `expressive` adds the air layer to `sheet` and the contour to `in`, never more voices.
Why: Sound scales with the site's motion so a calm brief stays calm in both.
Gate: manual: checklists/pre-delivery.md

### R-SND-09 Activation and silence
MUST. The module creates or resumes its audio context only inside a trusted `pointerdown` or `keydown`, or adopts the one `enhance.js` creates synchronously in the click that turns sound on or auditions a cue; never on load. A cue plays only while the context is `running`; otherwise it is dropped, never queued or replayed, so after a navigation the page stays silent until the first click or key press. A hidden tab, and turning sound off, cut every sounding and scheduled voice at once (`off` itself plays first). A failed import leaves the toggle truthful and the page working. Always silent: a count, a validation error, navigation and view transitions, loading, a toast appearing, a video starting, a disabled control.
Why: Browsers do not count hover or scroll as a gesture; a sound that arrives late is worse than none.
Gate: manual: checklists/pre-delivery.md

### R-SND-10 The same in every locale
MUST. Every locale and writing direction hears the same cues with the same meanings: no speech, no culture-specific instrument, no stereo position (right-to-left mirrors the layout, not the sound).
Why: Sound is part of the brand, and a cue that changed meaning by language would be a translation bug nobody can see.
Gate: manual: checklists/new-locale.md

### R-SND-11 The style guide auditions every cue
MUST. The style guide's foundations page has a Sound section listing every cue of R-SND-03 with when it plays and what it sounds like, each with a Play button (`button[data-sound-demo="<cue>"]`, shown only with the script) that plays that cue once through the production module without turning sound on. A new cue gets a row before it ships.
Why: Sound cannot be reviewed from a table of numbers; whoever changes it has to hear it.
Gate: manual: checklists/new-component.md
```
